# Product Vision

## Problem

The problem this app would solve is providing an alternative for reporting emergencies to the conventional one - calling the emergency number (112 in Bulgaria).

## Target Users

- People with auditory/vocal disabilities
- People that cannot access a mobile phone, but can access another type of device
- Kidnappees

## Goals

- A more orderly way to track national emergencies
- A more accessible way of reporting emergencies

## Types of emergencies
The following situations are based on the official Bulgarian 112
guidelines. [^1]
* **Medical emergency**
  * Obstruction/lack of breathing/heartbeat
  * Death
  * Critical physical trauma with blood loss
* **Abuse**
* A **minor missing** for more than 24 hours → report to the nearest local police station + a recent photograph
* **Natural disaster**
  * Hurricane
  * Earthquake
  * Flood
  * Fire
  * Snow storm
* **Catastrophy or other contamination**
  * Radiation
  * Road traffic accident
  * Industrial accident
  * Suspicion for anthrax or similar biological agents <br>

## Success Metrics

- A functional platform allowing reporting emergencies
